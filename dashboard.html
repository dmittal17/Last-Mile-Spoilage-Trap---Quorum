🎯 Demo Scenarios — Judge Guide

01 Split 180 meals across riders and shelters 🚀 Load Demo
Creates 7 riders, 3 shelters near Pune, dispatches 180 cooked meals (75 min shelf life). Watch the engine split the batch across riders and shelters without overfilling any.
02 Tight expiry batch is refused ⏱️ Tight Batch
Injects a 40-meal batch with 12 min shelf life. All shelters are too far for safe delivery — the engine emits UNPLACED rather than risk spoilage.
03 Ghost rider auto-released 👻 Ghost Rider
Advances time 6 min without sending heartbeats for one rider. The engine detects the ghost, releases their lease, and reassigns the cargo to another rider.
04 Direct donation fills shelter mid-transit 📦 Direct Donation
First pickup a lease, then click Direct Donation to fill the destination shelter. Watch the engine reroute the in-transit rider to a fallback shelter.
05 Shelter closes while in transit 🚫 Close Shelter
With an in-transit rider, close their destination shelter. The engine reroutes or splits the load across fallback shelters. If none exist, STRANDED is emitted.
